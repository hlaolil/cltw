What we offer

Calvary Life Tutors offers material and mentoring for short courses — the Certificate in Christian Discipleship, Diploma in Leadership, Advanced Diploma in Ministry and Higher Diploma in Shepherding — and for students registered for a Bachelor of Ministry. Courses are offered by Bible Schools (tuition centres) in phases of 80 credits each.

To earn a formal qualification, students present their CLT credits for Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) at an RPL institution. CLT courses themselves are unaccredited short courses.

We serve Evangelical, Charismatic and Pentecostal churches and hold to the fundamental principles of Christian doctrine. See our Statement of Faith.
